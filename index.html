HelpDesk Triage AI Live demo

Service desk · ITIL triage · visible rules

Paste an IT ticket and the page suggests a category, an ITIL impact × urgency priority, a first-response draft, and the next checks a first-line analyst would run. The rules are visible, so you can see which phrases fired. An optional second opinion with your own API key is available; the demo works without one.

  • Ten sample tickets with tests for category and priority, including the difference between one locked account (usually P3) and a whole-floor outage (P1).
  • No backend. Static page, free to try.

Reset & Phish Check Live demo

Password-reset playbook · phishing flags

Two first-line tools on one page: a password-reset identity-verification playbook that writes a ticket note you can copy, and a rule-based phishing email checker. Red flags stop a reset from being marked complete. Lookalike domains, mismatched From/Reply-To, urgent language and odd links are explained in plain language.

  • Built-in clean-reset and red-flag-call examples, plus four phishing samples with tests.
